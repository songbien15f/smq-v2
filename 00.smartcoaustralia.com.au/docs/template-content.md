Detailed Shop Drawings
Our highly skilled Vietnamese drafting office produces detailed shop drawings in Tekla — the model the factory builds from, checked before a single cut is made.
Workshop Inspection
Our own QC inspectors are in the workshop protecting structural integrity and specification compliance — preventing the costly rework and delays that happen when nobody is watching.
Trial Assembly

How It Works
Five steps. No unknowns.
The risk in offshore fabrication is the unknown. Our process is built to remove it — every step is documented, inspected and reported back to you.
1
Design & Engineering
Your drawings, or a full structural design from concept by certified Australian engineers.
2
Tekla Shop Drawings
Detailed shop drawings modelled in Tekla and issued for your review before fabrication starts.
3
Fabrication
Manufacture in a modern Vietnamese factory matched to your tonnage, with our team on site.
4
Inspection & QA
In-process QC, trial assembly where needed, and independent third-party inspection on request.
5
Door-to-Door Delivery
Protective packing, optimised loading, shipping and paperwork — delivered to your site, ready to erect.

Compliance & Quality
Documented proof, not promises
Every Smart Solutions Australia product is fully compliant with Australian standards, with a complete QA pack available for your project. Raw materials are tested in an ILAC-certified laboratory, and welders are certified by SGS to ISO 9606-1.
Inspection Test Plan (ITP) Hold points witnessed through fabrication
WPS, PQR & WPR Welding procedure and performance records
Mill Certificates & Test Reports Full material certification for every heat
Material Traceability Report CC3 traceability, Steelwork Compliance Australia
Dimensional & Weld Visual Reports Checked against the Tekla model and drawings
Surface & Painting Inspection Including hot-dip galvanising to AS/NZS 4680
NDT & Production Test Reports Available where your specification requires them
Independent Third-Party Inspection SGS, TUV and others arranged on request
Management systems: ISO 9001:2015 quality, ISO 14001:2015 environmental and ISO 45001:2018 occupational health & safety — applied across our offices and partner factories, with a published Modern Slavery Policy aligned to the Modern Slavery Act 2018 (Cth).
